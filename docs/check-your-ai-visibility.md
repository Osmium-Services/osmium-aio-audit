**AIO Audit** checks how well AI answer engines can find and cite your site. It looks for things like blocked AI crawlers and missing organisation and structured data, and lists the fixes in priority order.

## Run an audit

1. Open **AIO Audit** and press **Run audit**.
2. Keep the page open until it finishes.

## Read the results

- The **Site score** out of 100 sums it up.
- **Priority Fixes** lists each issue, how many pages it affects and how to fix it.
- **What Was Checked** shows every check and its result.

**Runs** lists past audits with the change **since the previous run**, so you can track whether your fixes help.

## Settings

**Most pages per run** and **Pause between requests** control how hard the audit works. **Crawl address** is only needed if the server cannot reach its own public address, so leave it blank normally.
